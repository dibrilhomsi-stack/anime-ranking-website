Top Anime to watch

1. Naruto Shippuden/Naruto
Naruto follows Naruto Uzumaki, a spirited ninja ostracized by his village because he hosts the Nine-Tails Fox, a powerful demon that attacked the village years ago. Naruto dreams of becoming Hokage, the strongest ninja and leader of his village, to earn recognition and protect those he cares about. In Naruto Shippuden, Naruto returns after training for several years, facing stronger enemies, uncovering secrets about his past, and working to bring peace to the ninja world. The series blends action, adventure, and emotional storytelling, exploring themes of friendship, perseverance, identity, and the struggle between vengeance and redemption.
2. One Piece
One Piece follows Monkey D. Luffy, a young pirate with the ability to stretch like rubber after eating a Devil Fruit, as he sails the seas in search of the legendary treasure “One Piece” to become the Pirate King. Along the way, he gathers a diverse crew, the Straw Hat Pirates, and faces powerful enemies, dangerous islands, and formidable pirate crews. The series blends adventure, comedy, action, and fantasy, exploring themes of friendship, freedom, ambition, and the pursuit of dreams.
3. Shingeki no Kyojin
Attack on Titan is set in a world where humanity is on the brink of extinction, living within massive walled cities to protect themselves from gigantic humanoid creatures known as Titans. The story follows Eren Yeager, his adoptive sister Mikasa, and friend Armin as they join the Scout Regiment to fight Titans after their city is destroyed. As the series unfolds, they uncover political conspiracies, hidden truths about the Titans, and the dark history of their world, blending intense action, suspense, and moral complexity. Themes include freedom, survival, revenge, and the cost of human ambition.
4. Dragon Ball Z
Dragon Ball Z follows Goku and his friends as they defend Earth and the universe from increasingly powerful villains, including aliens, androids, and magical beings. The series features intense battles, the pursuit of Dragon Balls — mystical orbs that grant any wish — and characters constantly training to surpass their limits. It blends action, adventure, and comedy, exploring themes of friendship, perseverance, self-improvement, and the eternal struggle between good and evil.
5. Fullmetal Alchemist brotherwood
Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood follows Edward and Alphonse Elric, two brothers who commit the taboo of human transmutation in an attempt to bring their mother back to life, resulting in Edward losing an arm and a leg and Alphonse losing his entire body. Using alchemy, they embark on a journey to restore their bodies by finding the legendary Philosopher’s Stone, uncovering deep political conspiracies, dark secrets about alchemy, and a threat to the entire world. The series blends action, adventure, fantasy, and emotional storytelling, exploring themes of sacrifice, morality, brotherhood, redemption, and the consequences of human ambition.
6. Hunter x hunter
Hunter × Hunter follows Gon Freecss, a young boy who discovers that his absentee father is a legendary Hunter, a licensed elite adventurer. Determined to find him, Gon embarks on a journey to become a Hunter himself, making friends like Killua, Kurapika, and Leorio along the way. Together, they face dangerous creatures, complex tournaments, and morally challenging adversaries. The series blends adventure, fantasy, and strategy, exploring themes of friendship, ambition, courage, and the ethical complexities of power and human nature.
7. Bleach
Bleach follows Ichigo Kurosaki, a teenager who gains the powers of a Soul Reaper — a death god tasked with protecting the living world from evil spirits called Hollows. As Ichigo balances his everyday life with his new responsibilities, he battles increasingly powerful enemies, uncovers hidden truths about the spirit world, and protects his friends and family. The series blends action, supernatural elements, and adventure, exploring themes of courage, duty, friendship, and the struggle between good and evil.
8. Death Note
Death Note follows Light Yagami, a highly intelligent high school student who discovers a mysterious notebook called the Death Note, which allows him to kill anyone by writing their name in it. Driven by a desire to rid the world of criminals, Light becomes a vigilante known as Kira, attracting the attention of the enigmatic detective L. The series is a psychological thriller that blends cat-and-mouse strategy, moral dilemmas, and supernatural elements, exploring themes of justice, power, morality, and the corrupting influence of absolute authority.
9. Jojo's Bizarre Adventure
JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure is a multi-generational saga following the Joestar family, each member nicknamed “JoJo,” as they confront supernatural threats, powerful villains, and bizarre phenomena. Each part of the series features different protagonists, settings, and conflicts, often involving Stands, manifestations of their fighting spirit with unique abilities. The series is known for its stylized art, inventive battles, and over-the-top storytelling, exploring themes of courage, family legacy, friendship, and the struggle between good and evil.
10. Code Geass
Code Geass follows Lelouch Lamperouge, a brilliant exiled prince of the Holy Britannian Empire, who gains a mysterious power called Geass, allowing him to command anyone to obey his orders. Using this power, Lelouch adopts the identity of Zero and leads a rebellion against Britannia to seek revenge, overthrow the empire, and create a better world for his sister Nunnally. The series blends mecha battles, political intrigue, and psychological strategy, exploring themes of power, morality, revenge, sacrifice, and the consequences of one’s choices.
11. Gurren Lagan
Gurren Lagann is a mecha action anime set in a dystopian future where humans live underground, oppressed by beastmen who control the surface. The story follows Simon and Kamina, two young men who discover a small mecha, “Lagann,” and use it to fight their way to the surface. Joined by Yoko and other allies, they form Team Gurren to battle the oppressive Spiral King and later face cosmic-scale threats. The series is renowned for its over-the-top action, inspirational themes, and escalating scale, exploring ideas of courage, friendship, belief in oneself, and breaking through limitations.
12. Fairy Tail
Fairy Tail is a fantasy adventure anime that follows Natsu Dragneel, a fiery Dragon Slayer wizard, and his friends from the Fairy Tail guild, one of the most famous guilds in the magical kingdom of Fiore. The series chronicles their missions, battles against dark guilds and powerful enemies, and the bonds they form with each other. Central themes include friendship, loyalty, courage, and the importance of found family, with a mix of high-stakes magical battles, humor, and heartwarming moments.
13. Demon slayer
Demon Slayer follows Tanjiro Kamado, a kind-hearted boy whose family is slaughtered by demons, leaving his sister Nezuko transformed into a demon. Determined to avenge his family and find a cure for Nezuko, Tanjiro becomes a Demon Slayer, joining a corps dedicated to hunting demons. Alongside other Demon Slayers, he faces increasingly powerful enemies while uncovering the mysteries behind the demon epidemic. The series blends action, fantasy, and emotional storytelling, exploring themes of family, perseverance, compassion, and the battle between good and evil.
14. Blue lock
Blue Lock is a sports anime focused on soccer, following Yoichi Isagi, a high school forward who joins a controversial training program called Blue Lock. The program is designed to create the world’s best striker for the Japanese national team by pitting 300 talented forwards against each other in a high-stakes survival-of-the-fittest environment. Players face intense physical, technical, and psychological challenges, where only the strongest and most selfish will succeed. The series explores ambition, competition, ego, teamwork, and the pursuit of greatness in a cutthroat sporting environment.
15. Jujutsu Kaisen
Jujutsu Kaisen follows Yuji Itadori, a high school student with extraordinary physical abilities who becomes involved in the world of curses — malevolent spiritual entities born from human negative emotions. After swallowing a powerful cursed object, the finger of the legendary curse Ryomen Sukuna, Yuji gains immense power but becomes a target for sorcerers and curses alike. He enrolls in Tokyo Jujutsu High, training under experienced sorcerers to fight curses and protect innocent people. The series blends supernatural action, dark fantasy, and intense battles, exploring themes of courage, friendship, sacrifice, and the moral complexities of power.
16. Kuroko's basket
Kuroko’s Basketball follows Seirin High School’s basketball team as they strive to become the best in Japan. Central to the story is Tetsuya Kuroko, a seemingly invisible player whose unique passing and misdirection skills make him a formidable asset, and Taiga Kagami, a naturally talented power player. Together, they face the Generation of Miracles, a group of elite middle school players now scattered across rival high schools. The series blends sports action, teamwork, rivalry, and personal growth, emphasizing strategy, determination, and the bonds formed through competition.
17. Fruits basket
Fruits Basket follows Tohru Honda, a kind-hearted high school girl who, after losing her mother, ends up living with the mysterious Soma family. She soon discovers that certain members of the Soma family are cursed to transform into animals of the Chinese zodiac whenever they are hugged by someone of the opposite sex. As Tohru gets to know each family member, she helps them confront their personal traumas, break free from the curse, and find happiness. The series blends romance, drama, and comedy, exploring themes of acceptance, healing, love, and the importance of family and friendship.
18. Haikyuu
Haikyuu!! follows Shoyo Hinata, a short but determined boy who dreams of becoming a top volleyball player despite his height disadvantage. After joining Karasuno High School’s volleyball team, he forms a dynamic partnership with Tobio Kageyama, a talented setter, and together they strive to compete at the national level. The series focuses on teamwork, perseverance, and personal growth, blending intense sports action with humor and heartfelt character development.
19. Eighty six
Eighty Six is a sci-fi military anime set in the Republic of San Magnolia, a nation at war with the autonomous drone army of the Empire of Giad. While the government claims the war is fought by unmanned machines, it is revealed that the so-called “unmanned” drones are actually piloted by a persecuted group of humans known as the Eighty-Six. The story follows Shinei “Shin” Nouzen, a skilled commander of the 86th unit, and Vladilena “Lena” Milizé, a government officer who seeks to understand and help the 86. The series explores war, discrimination, sacrifice, and the human cost of conflict, blending intense mecha battles with emotional and ethical storytelling.
20. My hero academia
My Hero Academia is set in a world where most of humanity possesses superpowers called “Quirks.” The story follows Izuku Midoriya, a Quirkless boy who dreams of becoming a hero like his idol All Might. After inheriting All Might’s power, he enrolls in U.A. High School, a prestigious academy for aspiring heroes, where he trains, faces villains, and learns what it truly means to be a hero. The series blends action, adventure, and coming-of-age themes, exploring courage, friendship, responsibility, and the struggle to protect others.
21. Promised Neverland
The Promised Neverland follows Emma, Norman, and Ray, three exceptionally intelligent orphans living in the seemingly idyllic Grace Field House orphanage. Their lives take a dark turn when they discover that the orphanage is actually a farm where children are raised to be fed to demons. Determined to survive, they devise a daring escape plan, using their wits and teamwork to outsmart their caretakers and uncover the truth about the outside world. The series blends thriller, mystery, and psychological suspense, exploring themes of friendship, survival, intelligence, and the loss of innocence.
22. Seven deadly sins
The Seven Deadly Sins follows Elizabeth Liones, a princess on a quest to find the legendary group of knights known as the Seven Deadly Sins, who were accused of plotting to overthrow the kingdom. She teams up with Meliodas, the leader of the Sins, and together they seek to clear the group’s name, confront corrupt Holy Knights, and protect the kingdom from dark forces. The series blends fantasy, adventure, action, and humor, exploring themes of friendship, loyalty, redemption, and the struggle between good and evil in a richly magical world.
23. Assassination Classroom
Assassination Classroom follows Class 3-E at Kunugigaoka Junior High, a group of academically struggling students tasked with assassinating their new teacher, Koro-sensei, a mysterious, superpowered being who has destroyed part of the moon and threatens to destroy Earth within a year. Despite his threat, Koro-sensei genuinely cares for his students, teaching them valuable lessons in academics, life, and personal growth while they attempt to kill him. The series blends comedy, action, and heartfelt drama, exploring themes of education, self-improvement, mentorship, and the bonds between teacher and students.
24. Tokyu Ghoul
Tokyo Ghoul follows Ken Kaneki, a college student who survives a deadly encounter with a ghoul, a humanoid creature that survives by eating humans. After a risky surgery, Kaneki becomes a half-ghoul, struggling to reconcile his human life with his new ghoul identity. He must navigate the dangerous world of ghouls, evade human investigators, and come to terms with his dual nature. The series blends dark fantasy, horror, and action, exploring themes of identity, morality, survival, and what it means to be human in a brutal, morally complex world.
25. Black clover
Black Clover follows Asta and Yuno, two orphans in a world where magic is everything. Yuno is a prodigy with immense magical talent, while Asta is born without any magic but compensates with incredible physical strength and determination. Both aim to become the Wizard King, the strongest mage in the Clover Kingdom. The series blends fantasy, action, and adventure, exploring themes of perseverance, rivalry, friendship, and overcoming limitations in a richly magical world.
26. Dr stone
Dr. Stone is a science-based adventure anime set in a world where humanity has mysteriously turned to stone for thousands of years. The story follows Senku Ishigami, a genius teenager determined to rebuild civilization through the power of science. With his knowledge, he awakens friends, creates technology from scratch, and battles rival factions to restore human society. The series blends adventure, problem-solving, and humor, exploring themes of ingenuity, perseverance, and the transformative power of scientific knowledge.
27. Classroom of The Elite
Classroom of the Elite is a psychological thriller set in Advanced Nurturing High School, an elite institution where students are ranked and rewarded based on merit and intelligence rather than conventional grades. The story follows Kiyotaka Ayanokoji, a seemingly ordinary student in Class 1-D, who hides extraordinary intelligence and manipulative skills. As he navigates complex social dynamics, exams, and class rivalries, Kiyotaka uncovers the dark truths behind the school’s system. The series explores themes of manipulation, ambition, social hierarchy, and the nature of true talent, blending suspense, strategy, and psychological intrigue.
28. Sword Art Online
Sword Art Online is a sci-fi fantasy anime that follows Kirito, a skilled gamer who becomes trapped in a virtual reality MMORPG along with thousands of other players. The catch: if a player dies in the game, they die in real life. Kirito must navigate dangerous game worlds, form alliances, and confront powerful enemies to survive and find a way to escape. The series explores virtual reality, love, friendship, courage, and the blurred lines between real and digital life, blending action, adventure, and emotional drama.
29. Violet evergarden
Violet Evergarden follows Violet, a young former soldier who, after a brutal war, struggles to understand human emotions and the meaning of love. She takes a job as an Auto Memory Doll, a professional ghostwriter who helps people convey their feelings through letters. Through her work, Violet embarks on a journey of self-discovery, gradually learning to process her trauma, connect with others, and understand the words “I love you” that were the last words of her mentor, Major Gilbert. The series blends emotional drama, beautiful animation, and touching stories, exploring themes of grief, healing, and the transformative power of communication and empathy.
30. Konosuba
Konosuba is a comedic fantasy anime that follows Kazuma Satou, a shut-in gamer who dies in a ridiculous accident and is given a chance to be reincarnated in a fantasy world. He forms a dysfunctional adventuring party with the goddess Aqua, the explosive mage Megumin, and the masochistic crusader Darkness. Together, they take on quests and monsters, but their combined incompetence often leads to hilarious misadventures rather than heroic triumphs. The series satirizes typical isekai and RPG tropes, blending comedy, fantasy, and parody to deliver a lighthearted, chaotic adventure.
